What NSW property sales actually look like

Pulled the 2024 annual file (14 MB → 53 weekly zips → per-LGA .DAT), parsed and de-duplicated every sale in the browser. 193,837 real transactions — the ground-truth label the model regresses against. Prices are contract prices; dates are contract dates.

De-dup is mandatory. Weekly files reissue sales for weeks after registration, so a raw sum double-counts. Before dedup Merrylands showed 12,558 sales; keyed on district+dealing-number it drops to 983. 22,955 duplicate rows removed here (11% of raw).
