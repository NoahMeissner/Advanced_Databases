Data reconnaissance · 3 NSW sources

What's actually in the three datasets

Goal: forecast how a single address out- or under-performs its suburb median over three years, driven by committed changes nearby. Below is what each source really holds — fields, granularity, how you get it, and where it plugs into the model.

The three sources

Ground truth

Valuer General property sales

nsw.gov.au · Bulk Property Sales Information Service

Fields
price, date, address, land size, type
History
bulk 1990+ · search 2001+
Grain
individual sale
Access
weekly bulk files
Licence
PSI for commercial

Role: the label. Address-level sale price is the y you regress suburb-relative performance against. CC BY-NC-ND for non-commercial; commercial use needs a PSI licence (email vnswprivacy@dpie.nsw.gov.au).

Amenity

NSW Points of Interest

maps.six.nsw.gov.au · ArcGIS REST · NSW_POI/MapServer/0

Features
145,779
Fields
poitype, poiname, geom, dates
Grain
point (GDA94 / 4283)
Access
open REST, no key
Cap
1,000 rows/query

Role: amenity proximity. 4,068 education points (schools→catchment proxy), 582 railway stations, plus parks, worship, childcare, shops. Query by poitype or bbox; paginate past the 1k cap.

Transport demand

Opal Tap (patronage)

opendata.transport.nsw.gov.au · opal-patronage

Fields
date, tap-on / tap-off counts
Range
Jan 2020 →
Grain
commercial centre, daily
Refresh
15 min (2-day) · full every 4d
Access
login to download

Role: realised demand trend, not timetable. CC-BY but download is gated behind a portal login. Watch: aggregated by centre, not per-station — coarse for address-level features.
